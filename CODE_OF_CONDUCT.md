# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ATDE a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and professional
- Assume defensive, lawful intent in discussions
- Do not request or share guidance for illegal offensive operations

## Enforcement

Maintainers may reject contributions or ban participants who violate this Code or [docs/LEGAL.md](docs/LEGAL.md).

Report issues to the maintainer via GitHub `@theworker02`.
