| Mute | `#8B9AAB` | Secondary text |

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least the radius of the inner sensor circle. Do not stretch, recolor to purple/neon glow, or place on busy photography without a dark slate panel.

## Name

- **Product:** ATDE (Autonomous Threat Disruption Engine)
